Nissan Skyline R34

The Nissan Skyline GT-R R34 is an iconic Japanese sports car produced from 1999 to 2002, powered by the twin-turbo RB26DETT engine and advanced ATTESA E-TS Pro AWD system. Its aggressive design, sharp handling, and strong tuning potential made it a legend in motorsport and JDM culture, remaining highly sought-after today for its performance technology, and unmistakable presence.
