FAQ

Q: What can I do in my virtual garage?
A: You can register, create, and manage your vehicles, View performance charts, and remove cars whenever you want.

Q: How do the performance graphs work?
A: The platform generates dynamometer-style charts using Chart.js, based on the technical data entered by the user.

Q: Do I need an account to use the platform?
A: Yes. Login and Registration are required to access your garage, the forum, and all main features.

Q: Can I buy or sell car parts on the platform?
A: No. A parts marketplace is not part of the project’s current scope.

Q: Is there a mobile app?
A: No. The project does not include a native mobile application at this time..

Q: Can I upload photos of my car?
A: Not yet. The current version of the platform does not support photo uploads.
