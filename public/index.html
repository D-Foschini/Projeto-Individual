About Us

Shakai Club is a space built for car enthusiasts who want to showcase and customize their own rides in their own style. Inspired by the iconic Japanese car culture of the 90s, our platform connects gearheads from all around the world. Created in Brazil in 2025, Shakai Club was made by enthusiasts, for enthusiasts — a place where you can share your build, explore other creations, and be part of a global community that lives and breathes cars. Whether you're into JDM legends, modern performance machines, or unique personal projects, this is where your passion finds a home.

User Generated Graphs

Our site lets you generate a custom dyno graph for your car in seconds. Just enter your engine data—such as horsepower, torque, or boost levels, and the system automatically plots a detailed performance curve. It’s a quick and accurate way to visualize how your setup behaves across the RPM range, helping you tune, compare, and understand your car’s power delivery.
